What can a stranger build from your Instagram?

Pick your own Instagram data export (the JSON ZIP from "Download your information"). Everything is read on this laptop.

What I will read

  • Your profile info file
  • The lists of your posts, stories and reels
  • The photos and videos those lists point to

What I will never open

  • Your messages
  • Your contacts and searches
  • Ad data, and anything else in the export

I never ask for your password, an OTP or your Instagram login. Nothing is saved after you close the app.

Optional: your voice

Read one sentence aloud (about 8 seconds) so I can tell your voice from other people's in reels. It stays in memory and is dropped when the run ends.
